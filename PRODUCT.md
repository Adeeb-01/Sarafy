# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Store employees working quickly in a brightly lit grocery environment. Arabic is the primary language, with full English support and correct RTL/LTR direction per language.

## Product Purpose
An entirely offline grocery operations tool that lets employees maintain USD prices and costs, convert shelf prices to SYP, record sales and expenses, issue invoices, and understand revenue and profit. Success means pricing and selling remain fast, legible, reliable, and recoverable without a network.

## Brand Personality
Practical, trustworthy, fast.

## Anti-references
No retail marketplace styling, promotional banners, decorative dashboards, excessive glass layers, or consumer checkout patterns. Glass is reserved for controls and navigation where depth improves grouping. The product must never imply that it sells online.

## Design Principles
1. Put the converted SYP price first.
2. Keep global pricing controls within one tap.
3. Prefer familiar controls and predictable feedback.
4. Make destructive and data-replacement actions explicit.
5. Preserve employee flow across both Arabic and English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast for bright-store use, 44px minimum touch targets, semantic labels, scalable text, reduced decorative motion, and mirrored layouts with correct numeral and content alignment.
