# Sarafy Design System

## Direction
Laminated price placards under bright market lighting. A deep berry-and-ink command field supports one functional frosted lens for exchange controls. Aqua marks live converted value and fluorescent lime appears only on immediate actions. Content remains calm, solid, and highly legible.

## Typography
Cairo across Arabic and English for a consistent bilingual rhythm. Prices use tabular figures and Cairo Black. Screen titles are 24px, card titles 16px, body 14px, supporting labels 12px.

## Components
Controls use 12–14px radii. Cards use a single subtle shadow without decorative borders. Pills are reserved for categories and selected filters. Motion is 160–220ms and communicates state only.

## Layout
16px phone gutters, 20px tablet gutters, 8px spacing rhythm, 44px minimum touch targets, and responsive two-column catalog cards on phones.

## Sales Analytics
The dashboard uses dense, high-contrast operational blocks rather than decorative KPI cards. Revenue, gross profit, expenses, and net profit have stable semantic colors. Trends remain simple native bars, while invoices and rankings use compact rows optimized for quick scanning in a bright store.
